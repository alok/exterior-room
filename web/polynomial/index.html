Orientation convention

Right-handed display basis X = −b, Y = a+c, Z = c−a, with e_x × e_y = e_z; local camera forward is −e_z. ZX turns about +Y, YZ about +X, XY about +Z. The existing ordered camera factorization is C = R_ZX(θ) R_YZ(φ) R_XY(ψ), equivalent to Euler YXZ. The rightmost factor acts first on column vectors. After ZX, the YZ and XY plane labels use the successively rotated camera basis. Drag changes the first two factors. These factors do not commute.

Eye at (0,0,0), the zero polynomial: no unique root or projective point; radial projection is undefined. Each line of sight is one class of nonzero forms; k·(a,b,c) share a ray, and ±(a,b,c), with the same roots, lie opposite. Looking up, the upper nappe appears as the Klein disk.

What am I seeing?

Each triple is ax²+bx+c, drawn at (X,Y,Z) = (−b, a+c, c−a), so Δ = b²−4ac = X²+Z²−Y². Upper interior (Δ<0, a>0) is colored by |Δ|: one color per sheet Y²−X²−Z² = |Δ|, a level set preserved by SL₂(ℤ). Exterior (Δ>0) has real roots, the cone (Δ=0) a double root; the lower interior repeats the upper roots with opposite sign. A white ring marks the selection.

Sources and scope

Separate Lean-backed adaptation of the coefficient-cone construction in Katherine Stange’s talk and Starscape / Lattice viewer, with pinned FareyVision modules. Source depth uses transformed trace/other-point catalogues and moves upper stars onto a variable-height disk. Its cone extends to depth R above and −0.3R below; its cutting-plane adapter rescales existing tessellation to source bounds, without claiming the original pixel mesh. Canonical ball retains its symmetric framing and fixed y=4 overlay. Entering Source from a smaller crop chooses depth 16; the original viewer defaults to 48.

Source position, radius and opacity formulas are checked against 48 offline evaluations of the deployed numerical code. Whole radial fade, screen attenuation, nonprimitive fade-out and plane emphasis are distinct. Selection rings remain independent of body crop. Source plane members use body emphasis; the extra member rings belong to Canonical mode. The original source’s screen, cone and plane do not interpolate with Project/Lift. Palette, materials, mesh sampling, camera framing and input routes still differ; no pixel equality is claimed.

Interior color uses Stange’s fixed logarithmic 3–240 |Δ| range, so equal Δ has equal palette position; nonprimitive multiples keep their own Δ. Our default maps it onto Viridis (Smith, van der Walt and Firing; CC0, license) from 0.2 to 1. Stange anchors uses her six anchors with OKLab interpolation. Exterior, cone and lower colors remain adaptations; lower-half coloring is an opt-in extension. Browser discs and native physical instances have different rasterization and materials.

Held source drags request temporary Lean catalogues at bounded quantized samples. Release validates the accepted action and matching catalogue; cancellation restores accepted state. Projection, preview scheduling, floating-point drawing and the 48 numerical fixtures are outside the unchanged 17 kernel-checked algebraic laws. FareyVision renderer modules retain provenance at ca809d2; new Lean code and proofs are this project’s work, not Stange’s. The deployed source is evaluated by the offline audit and is not bundled as an app dependency.
